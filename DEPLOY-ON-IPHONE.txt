BLOBS — NETLIFY IPHONE DEPLOY

1. In Netlify, open blobscardgame.
2. Open Deploys.
3. Choose Add new deploy / Deploy manually.
4. Upload this ZIP file.
5. Wait until the deploy says Published.
6. Open https://blobscardgame.netlify.app

The site files are at the ZIP root, including index.html.
This package currently provides the local three-player demo.
Online rooms still need a hosted multiplayer backend.
